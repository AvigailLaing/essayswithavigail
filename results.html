Here is the part worth sitting with. My most expensive package is $549. Gabi's merit aid came to 739 times that. The student at NYU Shanghai is getting 130 times the cost of the package, every year, for four years.

Merit scholarships are decided on essays. So is admission to the schools that hand them out. Grades and test scores are finished by senior year and there is nothing left to do about them, but the essay is still sitting there, unwritten, and it is the one piece of the application a student can still change.

These are figures students reported to me themselves, and every screenshot below is the message they came in. I cannot promise anyone a scholarship, and nobody honest can. What I can tell you is that this is where the money gets decided, and it is the part most families spend the least time on.
